About Lifeline

Quality care. Right at home. Building stronger communities across Vancouver Island.

Our mission

Lifeline Community Care and Home Services Inc. provides trusted, warm, and professional support that helps people live safely, comfortably, and independently at home.

We serve families, seniors, people with disabilities, facilities, and community partners with care that is respectful, reliable, and personal.

What guides us

Compassion Dignity Independence
  • Trained & background-checked carers you can trust in your home.
  • Flexible care plans that grow with changing needs.
  • Respectful, reliable service—compassionate people, on time.
